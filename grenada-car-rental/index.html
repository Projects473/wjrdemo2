Good to know

Included

  • Collision and liability insurance (the renter covers any deductible)
  • A full tank at pickup; return it at the same level
  • Automatic gearbox and right-hand drive

What to bring

  • Your driver's licence and a photo ID
  • A temporary Grenada driver's licence (EC$60, from the Central Police Station or the South St. George Police Station; we usually take you there)
  • A security deposit, paid by cash or card before pickup

Driving in Grenada

Grenada drives on the left. Roads are narrow and hilly in places, so a compact automatic hybrid makes island driving easy and keeps fuel costs down.

Rental terms

Personal use only, and only the renter may drive. No smoking, pets, racing or towing. Late returns are EC$40 per day unless agreed in advance. Read the full rental agreement (PDF).

Car rental questions

Do I need a local driving permit to rent a car in Grenada?

Yes. Visitors need a temporary Grenada driver's licence as well as their own licence. It costs EC$60 and you can get it at any time from the Central Police Station in St. George's or the South St. George Police Station. We usually take renters to the police station to get it.

Which side of the road do you drive on in Grenada?

On the left. All our rental cars are automatic, right-hand-drive hybrids, so there is no gear stick to manage.

How much does it cost to rent a car in Grenada?

Our daily rates start at EC$130 for rentals of 1 to 6 days, with lower rates for 7 days or more. Collision and liability insurance is included.

What do I need to bring?

Your driver's licence and a photo ID. A security deposit is paid by cash or card before pickup and returned when the car comes back in the same condition.
