Mt. Parnassus traditional breakfast

Taste the Caribbean's traditional breakfasts at our Saturday traditional breakfast, without leaving Grenada, the Isle of Spice. From Jamaica in the north to Trinidad and Tobago in the south.

Every Saturday from 7 am at D Bamboo, Mt. Parnassus Plantation

On the table

Sample ackee and saltfish, Jamaica's traditional dish, along with mackerel, coco bread, sorrel and much more.

Home of D Bamboo Breakfast

Come and enjoy our authentic traditional Grenada plantation breakfast at D Bamboo, "Home of D Bamboo Breakfast", located at Mt. Parnassus Plantation.

Traditional music

Enjoy Grenada's traditional music with the djembe drum, tamboo bamboo, shack and cow bell.
