Airport taxi questions

How much is a taxi from Grenada airport to Grand Anse?

From Maurice Bishop International Airport (MBIA) to the Grand Anse hotels, such as Siesta and the Umbrellas area, the fare is EC$60 / US$25 for 1 to 2 passengers. Each extra passenger is EC$10.

How much is a taxi from the airport to St. George's?

From MBIA to St. George's the fare is EC$70 / US$28 for 1 to 2 passengers. Each extra passenger is EC$10.

Will my driver meet me at arrivals?

Yes. Send your flight number and arrival date when you book, and your driver will be waiting at arrivals at Maurice Bishop International Airport.

Can you pick me up from the cruise port?

Yes. We pick up from the cruise port in St. George's and from hotels and villas across the island. Use the booking bar on the home page to see the rate from your pickup point.

Are the rates per person or per car?

Rates are per trip for 1 to 2 passengers. Each passenger after the first two is EC$10.
