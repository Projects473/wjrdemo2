QuestionsGrenada taxi, tours and rentals

Quick answers about airport taxis, island tours and renting a car in Grenada.

How much is a taxi from Grenada airport to Grand Anse?

From Maurice Bishop International Airport (MBIA) to the Grand Anse hotels, such as Siesta and the Umbrellas area, the fare is EC$60 / US$25 for 1 to 2 passengers. Each extra passenger is EC$10. Use the booking bar to see the rate for any destination.

Will my driver meet me at the airport?

Yes. Send your flight number when you book and your driver will be waiting at arrivals at MBIA. We also pick up from the cruise port in St. George's.

Do you offer tours for cruise ship passengers?

Yes. Garden of Eden and St. George's are half-day tours (4 hours), which suit a short time in port; Waterfalls, Chocolate Rum and Round de Island are whole days (7 hours). Tell us your ship's arrival and departure times when you book.

How much is a private island tour in Grenada?

A half-day tour (4 hours) is EC$370 / US$140 and a whole-day tour (7 hours) is EC$650 / US$245, for 1 to 2 people. Each additional person is EC$26 / US$10, up to 5 people per vehicle. Garden of Eden and St. George's are half-day tours; Waterfalls, Chocolate Rum and Round de Island are whole-day tours.

Do I need a local driving permit to rent a car in Grenada?

Yes. Visitors need a temporary Grenada driver's licence as well as their own licence. It costs EC$60 and you can get it at any time from the Central Police Station in St. George's or the South St. George Police Station. We usually take renters to the police station to get it.

Which side of the road do you drive on in Grenada?

On the left. All our rental cars are automatic, right-hand-drive hybrids, so there is no gear stick to manage.

Can I make a stop on the way?

Yes. Choose the number of stops in the booking bar and tell us where, for example to shop at IGA before going to your hotel. Each stop on the way is EC$20.
