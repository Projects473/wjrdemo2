Good to knowRental terms
at a glance

The short version of the WJR Taxi Tours & Auto Rentals rental agreement. Ask on WhatsApp if anything is unclear.

Full rental agreement (PDF)
“

What to bring

Your driver's licence and a photo ID.

“

Security deposit

Paid by cash or card before pickup, and returned when the car comes back in the same condition, less anything owed.

“

Insurance

Collision and liability included. The renter covers any deductible, and their own car insurance acts as primary cover.

“

Fuel and late returns

Full tank at pickup; return it at the same level. Late returns are EC$40 per day unless agreed in advance.

“

Who can drive

Personal use only, and only the renter may drive.

“

House rules

No smoking, no pets, no racing and no towing. Report any accident, theft or damage right away.
