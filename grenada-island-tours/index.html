Tour questions

How much is a private island tour in Grenada?

A half-day tour (4 hours) is EC$370 / US$140 and a whole-day tour (7 hours) is EC$650 / US$245, for 1 to 2 people. Each additional person is EC$26 / US$10.

Can cruise ship passengers book a tour?

Yes. Tours run as a half day or a whole day, so they fit around your time in port. Tell us your ship's arrival and departure times when you book, and we will pick you up from the cruise port in St. George's.

Are the tours private?

Yes. Each tour is just you and your group with your own driver, so you set the pace and can spend longer at the stops you enjoy most.

Where do tours start?

We pick you up from your hotel, villa or the cruise port and drop you back at the end of the tour.
